# CYBER + CAFÉ Website

A premium, modern, aesthetic website designed for **CYBER + CAFÉ**, merging the warm atmosphere of an artisanal coffee shop with the modern vibe of a cyber café and high-performance gaming space.

---

- **Main Background**: Warm Cream (`#FFF4E6`) — Dominant colour throughout
- **Header Background**: Coffee Brown (`#5C3825`)
- **Footer Background**: Dark Coffee (`#392719`)
- **Secondary Background**: Light Beige (`#F8EBDD`)
- **Accent Colour**: Caramel (`#C18A55`)
- **Main Text**: Dark Chocolate (`#392719`)

## ☕ Sections Overview

1. **Header / Navigation Bar**:
   - Sticky coffee-brown header with cream typography (**CYBER + CAFÉ**).
   - Smooth navigation: `Home`, `About Us`, `Venue`, `Facilities`, `Contact`.
   - Responsive mobile navigation drawer.

2. **Home / Hero Section**:
   - Main Heading: *"WHERE COFFEE MEETS TECHNOLOGY"*
   - Tagline: *"Work. Connect. Play. Relax."*
   - Interactive visual showcase combining coffee craftsmanship with modern workstation imagery.
   - Atmosphere pillars & filterable visual gallery with full-screen lightbox modal.

3. **About Us Section (`#about`)**:
   - Left empty with generous cream whitespace, ready for your custom story.

4. **Venue Section (`#venue`)**:
   - Left completely empty with cream whitespace, ready for venue details and pictures.

5. **Facilities Section (`#facilities`)**:
   - Left completely empty with cream whitespace, ready for facility specifications.

6. **Contact Section (`#contact`)**:
   - Beautiful editorial layout with large designated empty slots ready for:
     - Email address
     - Phone number
     - Café address
     - Social media links
   - Zero invented or fake contact info.
   - Interactive inquiry message box.

7. **Footer**:
   - Dark coffee-brown background (`#392719`) with cream text.
   - **CYBER + CAFÉ** branding, navigation links, and empty slots for social media and contact details.
